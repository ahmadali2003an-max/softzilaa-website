Process

Our Working Process

Project timelines vary depending on scope and complexity. Here's a general guide: Simple websites (1–2 weeks), Full business websites (2–4 weeks), E-commerce platforms (4–8 weeks), Branding packages (1–2 weeks), SEO campaigns (3–6 months for results), Social media management (ongoing). We provide detailed timelines in every proposal.

Our process follows 6 clear stages: 1) Discovery & Consultation — understanding your goals, audience, and requirements. 2) Strategy & Planning — detailed roadmap, timeline, and deliverables. 3) Design & Prototyping — creating visual concepts for your approval. 4) Development & Implementation — building the solution. 5) Testing & Quality Assurance — thorough testing. 6) Launch & Support — deployment and ongoing support.

Most projects include 2–3 rounds of revisions as standard. We start with detailed briefing to minimize revisions needed. Each revision round allows you to request changes, adjustments, and refinements. Our goal is your complete satisfaction, so we work with you until the final result meets your expectations.
